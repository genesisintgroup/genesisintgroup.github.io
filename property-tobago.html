Overview

A spacious two-storey residence in southwest Tobago

Built around 2012 on two contiguous freehold lots totalling 15,220 sq ft, this detached two-storey residence offers approximately 6,978 sq ft of floor area across a ground floor and a first floor, with four bedrooms (the master en-suite with a walk-in closet), three full bathrooms, two kitchens, a dining room, living room, family room, laundry and covered porches. Construction is rendered hollow blockwork on a concrete slab beneath a standing-seam roof, and the property is in good condition with split A/C units, burglar bars, cable, and 3,000 gallons of water storage with a pump system.

Carnbee is a relatively small settlement in the southwest of Tobago — the main tourist-oriented part of the island — close to a range of services and amenities. The ground floor has most recently been used as a private kindergarten and can easily be reconverted to residential space or a separate living accommodation.

Location

Carnbee, Tobago, Trinidad and Tobago

Carnbee sits in the southwest of Tobago, the main tourist-oriented part of the island, west of Scarborough and north of Lowlands. The property is on Richard Drive, off Sherwood Park Trace, about a quarter mile from the Auchenskeoch Buccoo Bay Road and roughly one mile from the Claude Noel Highway.
