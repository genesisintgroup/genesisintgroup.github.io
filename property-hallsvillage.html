Overview

A spacious five-bedroom home on Barbados' Platinum Coast

Built in the 2000s on a 3,420 sq ft freehold lot in Halls Village, St. James, this two-story home offers roughly 2,552 sq ft of living space across five bedrooms. The concrete block construction is finished with rendered and painted elevations beneath a pitched roof, with upvc sash windows, French doors, covered terraces, a solar water heater, air conditioning, and a private roof deck.

Halls Village is an established residential development in St. James — Barbados' famous 'Platinum Coast', home to Sandy Lane and Royal Westmoreland — with good access to local services and just a short drive from Holetown and the Warrens commercial hub.
